# S-I-R epidemic model (probabilistic)
The world is a 40x40 grid.
Cells can be SUSCEPTIBLE, INFECTED or RECOVERED.
A SUSCEPTIBLE cell becomes INFECTED with probability 0.3 if at least 1 neighbour is INFECTED.
An INFECTED cell becomes RECOVERED with probability 10%.
A RECOVERED cell remains RECOVERED.
Initially 2% of cells are INFECTED and 98% are SUSCEPTIBLE.
The seed is 7.
Simulate 200 steps.
